My Interests

Here’s a look at what inspires, motivates, and excites me both inside and outside the classroom.

💻 Technology & Innovation

I’ve always been fascinated by how technology shapes the world. I love learning about coding, AI, and how small projects can make big impacts. I enjoy experimenting with websites, interactive media, and digital design — using creativity to make something unique and functional.

🎨 Art & Creativity

Art allows me to express myself in ways words can’t. I enjoy sketching, digital illustration, and even photography. My creative side helps me stay imaginative in everything I do — from designing websites to coming up with fresh ideas for school projects.

🏀 Sports & Fitness

Staying active is important to me. I love basketball, running, and going to the gym — they help me stay disciplined, motivated, and focused. Sports also taught me teamwork, perseverance, and how to push through challenges both physically and mentally.

🎧 Music & Expression

Music has always been a big part of my life. It helps me relax, focus, and find creativity in everyday moments. Whether it’s R&B, hip-hop, or lo-fi, I love how music tells stories and connects people emotionally — sometimes better than words ever could.

🌱 Personal Growth & Reflection

Beyond my hobbies, I care deeply about becoming the best version of myself. I spend time reflecting, setting goals, and improving little by little every day. I believe in lifelong learning — not just in academics, but in understanding who I am and what I want to bring to the world.
